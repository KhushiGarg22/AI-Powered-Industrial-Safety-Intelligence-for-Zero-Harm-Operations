# 🦺 AI-Powered Industrial Safety Intelligence for Zero-Harm Operations

> **ET AI Hackathon 2.0 | Team ZeroHarm AI**

## 📖 Overview

AI-Powered Industrial Safety Intelligence for Zero-Harm Operations is an intelligent safety monitoring platform designed to improve workplace safety in hazardous industries. The system leverages Artificial Intelligence (AI), Computer Vision, Machine Learning, IoT, SCADA, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), Knowledge Graphs, and Geospatial Analytics to monitor industrial environments, detect hazards, predict potential risks, and support proactive decision-making.

Unlike conventional safety systems that operate independently, this platform integrates multiple industrial data sources—including IoT sensors, CCTV cameras, SCADA systems, permit-to-work records, maintenance logs, and historical incident reports—into a unified AI-powered ecosystem. By correlating real-time operational data, the system identifies compound risks, detects PPE violations using YOLOv11, predicts equipment failures, and generates intelligent safety recommendations to support Zero-Harm Operations.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Industrial accidents often occur due to delayed hazard detection, isolated monitoring systems, human errors, and the inability to correlate multiple risk factors in real time. Existing safety systems generate alerts independently, making proactive risk management difficult.

This project addresses these challenges by developing an integrated AI-powered industrial safety intelligence platform capable of real-time monitoring, predictive analytics, and automated emergency response.
